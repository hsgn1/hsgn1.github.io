About Us

Company Profile

Located in Dongying, Shandong Province, Dongying Erya Technology Co., Ltd. is a modern enterprise integrating R&D, production and sales of pigments and pigment additives. We have a professional R&D team, standardized production workshops and a complete quality inspection system.


For many years, we have focused on the pigment and additive industry, adhering to technological innovation and strict quality control. Our products are widely used in coatings, inks, plastics, rubber, building materials and other fields. Following the principle of integrity management, we have gained recognition from customers at home and abroad with stable quality, reasonable prices and considerate service.


In the future, we will continue to increase R&D investment and optimize product performance, striving to become a reliable supplier of pigments and additives in the industry.
